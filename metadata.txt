about=Raster Scribe is an independently maintained fork of Raster Tracer by Mikhail Kondratyev.
    Click along a line in an RGB raster to draw vector geometry. The fork adds live path previews,
    windowed raster sampling, saved preferences, color sampling and dense straight-line shortcuts,
    and support for QGIS 3.40+ and QGIS 4.
    It also revises cancellation, undo, snapping, and handling of invalid raster pixels.
    An editable MultiLineString or MultiCurve layer and a GDAL-readable RGB raster are required.
    Rotated or skewed raster mappings are unsupported.
    Original project: https://github.com/mkondratyev85/raster_tracer
