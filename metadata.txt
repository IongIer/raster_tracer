about=Raster Scribe is an independently maintained fork of Raster Tracer by Mikhail Kondratyev.
    Click along a line in an RGB raster to draw vector geometry. The fork adds live path previews,
    windowed raster sampling, saved preferences, color sampling and dense straight-line shortcuts,
    an optional form for finished lines with field creation, configurable controls
    with QGIS shortcut conflict warnings and a saved finish-form modifier,
    scratch-layer creation, and support for QGIS 3.40+ and QGIS 4.
    It also revises cancellation, undo, snapping, and handling of invalid raster pixels.
    Optional enhanced tracing follows contour continuity through interruptions, using more processing.
    Toggle it with E by default; the choice is saved and the standard tracer remains the default.
    A GDAL-readable RGB raster and an editable MultiLineString or MultiCurve layer are required;
    the plugin can create a temporary line layer matching the raster's CRS.
    Rotated or skewed raster mappings are unsupported.

    Original project: https://github.com/mkondratyev85/raster_tracer
